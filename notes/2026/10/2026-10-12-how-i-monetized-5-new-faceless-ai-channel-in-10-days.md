## Summary
A step-by-step framework for setting up, warming up, testing niches, and scaling automated faceless YouTube channels using AI tools and growth heuristics.

## Key Takeaways
- Warm up brand new YouTube accounts for at least one week by browsing organically, watching 1-2 hours of content daily, and interacting naturally to bypass shadowbans and establish platform trust.
- Evaluate potential niches using strict criteria: under 5 smaller channels with high view-to-subscriber ratios, zero channels with over 100k+ subscribers, no failing smaller channels, and a niche age under 6 months.
- Use a dedicated 'dummy account' trained by watching niche-specific videos to manipulate the YouTube homepage algorithm into showing endless competitor research ideas.
- Apply the Topic Transfer Method: take winning video topics from unrelated high-performing niches and adapt them to your own niche for high demand and low supply.
- Make your video duration roughly 1.5x longer than competitor videos to maximize watch time and capture algorithm favorability.
- Structure production via the 80/20 rule: spend 80% of your time on proven formats and 20% experimenting with new ideas.

## Actionable Frameworks
### Niche Testing Framework
A methodical process covering channel warmup, niche selection criteria, competitor outlier identification, and production testing of 5-10 videos before scaling or pivoting.

### Topic Transfer Method
The practice of identifying high-performing, viral video concepts from different, non-competing niches and successfully migrating them into your target niche.
