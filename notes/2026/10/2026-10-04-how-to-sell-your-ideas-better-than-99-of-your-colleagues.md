## Summary
By analyzing how Don Draper pitches the Kodak Carousel in Mad Men, communication trainer Shannon Jenkins reveals how to pitch ideas successfully by leveraging emotional resonance, storytelling, and curiosity instead of relying purely on logic.

## Key Takeaways
- Do not automatically give people the exact answer they expect or ask for; instead, look for a smarter, less obvious angle they may have missed.
- Avoid setting up an adversarial 'you versus them' debate when presenting ideas; instead, bring in third-party perspectives, experts, or customer data to foster curiosity rather than defensiveness.
- Do not rely solely on rational, logical arguments because people are motivated to act by how things make them feel.
- Help your audience experience an emotional shift or see things differently before introducing your new concept or product.
- When your idea has successfully landed, stop talking immediately and let the silence do the work of letting the concept sink in.

## Actionable Frameworks
### The Emotional Resonance Pitch Strategy
A methodology based on emotional communication where you connect a new idea or product to a deep human emotion (like nostalgia or a sense of belonging) rather than just listing technical features.

### The Third-Party Reframing Method
A persuasion technique where you challenge someone's initial brief or assumptions not by directly telling them they are wrong, but by sharing a lesson or perspective learned from an experienced mentor or third party.
