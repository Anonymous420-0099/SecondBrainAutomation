## Summary
Saad Khaja breaks down the exact 3-part sales engine and 7-component offer architecture used to scale a service business to 50 crores.

## Key Takeaways
- A sale relies on three core components: the Offer (what you sell), Distribution (who you sell to), and Sales (how you sell to them).
- To command high prices, your offer must be hyper-specific and directly target the customer's dream outcome rather than offering vague services.
- Use a value stack where every added layer directly counteracts and solves a specific obstacle or pain point your prospect faces.
- Always apply the 5-minute rule for inbound inquiries to maximize conversion probability before enthusiasm fades.
- In deep discovery, navigate the 'Three Gates of Hell' (Need, Timeline/Decision, and Budget/Anchor) using curiosity and belief-breaking questions instead of generic pitching.

## Actionable Frameworks
### The 7-Component Offer Stack
A methodology for structuring service offers by being hyper-specific, stacking value to solve obstacles, showing undeniable proof, reducing time/effort, minimizing risk, and adding urgency.

### The Three Gates of Hell
A deep discovery sales framework consisting of three qualifying gates: Need, Decision/Timeline, and Budget/Anchor, designed to filter out unqualified prospects.
