## Summary
By shifting from simply recounting history to structuring videos around strong hooks and a repeatable three-act narrative template driven by curiosity gaps, a creator broke out of view jail to achieve over 100,000 views per video.

## Key Takeaways
- Stop starting videos with generic self-introductions; instead, open with a compelling hook, a story, and a clear reason for the viewer to care within the first 10 seconds.
- Formulate an overarching curiosity gap by constantly asking 'what am I genuinely curious about and what happens if I pull the thread?'.
- Apply a three-act structure to your video scripts, ensuring sections systematically raise a question, complicate what viewers think they know, and deliver an insightful answer.
- Use comment or highlight features in your scriptwriting to label major beats, catalysts, and climaxes to maintain a tight narrative flow.
- Fix the core idea and question before spending hours on execution and polish, as a good video cannot rescue a bad underlying concept.

## Actionable Frameworks
### The Three-Act Video Template
A structural storytelling method where every section raises a question, complicates it by challenging assumptions, and finally answers it to maintain high viewer engagement.

### The Curiosity Gap Framework
A narrative approach that shifts content creation from simple recounting of history to exploring a compelling core question that drives the viewer to stick around for the answer.
