## Actionable Frameworks
### Motion Transfer
A workflow where motion and camera tracking are extracted from a reference video and re-targeted onto a target image character while preserving action fidelity.

### Objects Swap
A targeted video-editing technique that isolates specific elements or props in a video and replaces them with a tagged reference image using text prompts.
