## Summary
Practical strategies to bypass YouTube algorithm view suppression, including format bending, CPR, and publishing schedule formulas.

## Key Takeaways
- Toggle the 'Publish to subscriptions feed and notify subscribers' button on and off for every other video to test new audiences and force the algorithm to push content.
- Use the CPR (Cardio Pulmonary Resuscitation) method by alternating publishing status or video formats to jumpstart stalled channels.
- Leverage collaborations with established channels in your niche to cross-pollinate and jumpstart initial audience growth.
- Apply 'format bending' by taking a successful format from another channel or niche and re-engineering it for your own channel.
- Maintain consistent uploading of 1 to 5 videos a day for 8 to 12 consecutive days to hit the metrics required for algorithm push.
- Monitor monthly audience and watch behavior metrics to prevent casual viewer ratios from exceeding 50% and triggering viewbomb suppression.

## Actionable Frameworks
### The CPR Method
Alternating turning off and on the subscription feed notification for successive videos to regularly test new audiences and escape suppression.

### Format Bending
Taking an already proven high-performing content format from another channel or niche and adapting it to your own topic.

### The Push Formula
Maintaining a steady stream of uploads for 8 to 12 consecutive days while keeping average view duration (AVD), swipe ratio, and audience growth aligned.
