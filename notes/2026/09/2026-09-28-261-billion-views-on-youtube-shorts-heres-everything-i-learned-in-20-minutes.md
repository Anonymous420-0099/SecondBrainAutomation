## Summary
A granular breakdown of YouTube Shorts algorithm tiers from 0 to 100 million views, diagnosing specific metric bottlenecks and actionable fixes for each level.

## Key Takeaways
- Zero View Jail (0-100 views) is typically a trust score issue caused by account setup problems, banned IPs, or reused content; fix it by checking proxy settings and IP addresses.
- 30K View Jail usually stems from analytics issues like low swipe ratios (aim for >81.1%) or average view duration below video length.
- 50K-100K View Jail often happens during the test-and-rebalance period (lasting 8 days to 6 weeks) where you need to maintain consistency and validate your video's idea via competitor research.
- 1 Million View Jail is an ideation and TAM (Total Addressable Market) problem; your niche is too small or your idea needs to be broadened to target a wider audience.
- 10 Million+ View Jail represents a market cap ceiling; breaking past it requires broad, universally understandable storytelling and concepts that resonate globally.
- Optimize your CTAs (Call to Actions) by asking viewers to check out other videos on your channel rather than generic subscription requests.
