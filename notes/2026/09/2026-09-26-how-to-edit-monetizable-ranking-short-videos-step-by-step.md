## Summary
To avoid YouTube demonetization for reused content, ranking short videos must be edited transformatively using original voiceover commentary, dynamic pacing, custom visual effects, and persistent ranking overlays.

## Key Takeaways
- Prevent YouTube demonetization by ensuring compilation videos feature substantial personal commentary, unique audio, and transformative editing rather than raw re-uploads.
- Structure ranking Shorts around 30 seconds total duration (3-4 clips at ~7 seconds each plus voiceover gaps) for optimal retention on YouTube Shorts.
- Hook viewers immediately by placing the most shocking moment at the start with a quick zoom animation and shutter sound effect before introducing voice commentary.
- Record custom voiceovers with personal opinions on each clip rather than relying on generic AI text-to-speech tools, which increase demonetization risk.
- Maintain high audience retention by withholding the final outcome or ranking culmination until the end of the video.
- Apply visual polish using CapCut's HD quality enhancement, camera shake effects on emotional reactions, Vine boom audio cues, and bold yellow/green caption styling with black stroke outlines.

## Actionable Frameworks
### Transformative Short Editing Workflow
A 5-step video editing methodology for ranking Shorts: 1) Select a shocking visual hook, 2) Cut clips into 7-second segments with silenced commentary gaps, 3) Layer original audio commentary and sound effects (Vine boom/shutter click), 4) Create a persistent on-screen ranking list with consistent typography, and 5) Apply HD upscaling and dynamic captions.
